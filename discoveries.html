EARLY GALAXY

JWST: JADES-GS-z14-0

Webb has discovered the earliest known galaxy, existing just 290 million years after the Big Bang. Its extreme size and brightness defy standard cosmological structure models.

Redshift: z = 14.32 | Age: 13.5 Billion Years Ago
BLACK HOLE

EHT: Sgr A* Polarization

The Event Horizon Telescope has captured the first polarized light image of the Milky Way's central black hole, Sgr A*, revealing powerful spiral magnetic fields.

Instrument: EHT Interferometer | Feature: Polarized Accretion
SPACETIME HUM

NANOGrav: Cosmic Hum

Astronomers detected a low-frequency hum of gravitational waves rippling through spacetime, likely from supermassive black hole binaries merging across the universe.

Frequency: Nanohertz | Method: Pulsar Timing Arrays
ATMOSPHERE

JWST: LHS 475 b Transit

Webb completed high-precision transit spectroscopy of the rocky Earth-sized exoplanet LHS 475 b, charting molecular absorption features in its atmosphere.

Planet: Rocky Earth-sized | Method: Transmission Spectra
COSMIC WEB

Euclid: Cosmic Web Map

Euclid has captured wide-field images of billions of galaxies, tracing dark matter scaffolding and mapping the large-scale cosmic web structure across 10 billion years.

Scope: 1/3 of Celestial Sky | Mapping Target: Dark Matter Gaps
