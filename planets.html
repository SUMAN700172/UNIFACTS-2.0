OCEAN WORLD

Jupiter & Europa

Europa is squeezed and stretched by Jupiter's massive gravity fields, creating friction-based tidal heating that maintains a saline ocean beneath 20km of surface ice.

Heating Mode: Gravitational Tides | Ocean Depth: 100 km
RING RESONANCE

Saturn & Titan

Saturn is adorned with millions of orbiting ice particles shaped by shepherd moons, while Titan hosts a dense nitrogen atmosphere with active liquid methane lakes.

Ring Type: Ice Sheets | Titan Liquids: Liquid Methane
DECAYING SPIRAL

Mars & Phobos

Phobos is spiraling inwards towards Mars at 1.8 meters per century. Within 30 million years, it will cross the Roche limit and break apart to form a planetary ring.

Orbital Period: 7.6 Hours | Limit: Roche Disintegration
BARYCENTER TIDES

Earth & Luna

Our planet and tidally locked Moon orbit a mutual center of mass located 4,670 km from Earth's center, pulling ocean tides in a constant gravitational dance.

Center: Internal Barycenter | Lock: 1:1 Tidal Ratio
BINARY SYSTEM

Pluto & Charon

Charon is so massive compared to Pluto that their mutual center of mass lies in empty space between them. They wobble eternally around a true external barycenter.

Double Planet: Mutual Lock | Barycenter: External Void
